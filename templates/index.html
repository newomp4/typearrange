how this works
Drop a video. Whisper transcribes it locally with word-level timestamps. Words get grouped into caption phrases of 4–7 words and laid out in a tight collage. Important words use the black weight; articles and fillers use thin/italic. Animation snaps at a low framerate for the posterized motion-graphic feel.
